About me

I'm an Electrical Engineer with a growing interest in intelligent systems. My work has taken me through power engineering, industrial automation, software development, and machine learning. While the technologies change, the motivation stays the same: understand how complex systems work, then find better ways to build them.

On a more personal note, I'm shaped as much by the books I've read as the projects I've built. My blog is where those influences come together, exploring ideas beyond engineering, from technology and philosophy to the questions that simply refuse to leave my mind.
